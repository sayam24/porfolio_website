WBG/UWBG Photoconductive Switches

Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

Under the mentorship of Dr. Bikramjit Chatterjee, I conductued R&D experiments to test the effectiveness of doped GaN and AlN as photoconductive switches by capturing Raman and PL spectra as well as pulsed and steady-state IV responses of the devices. Key findings were summarized and presented in poster format, and a manuscript will hopefully be submitted by the end of the year.

Multicycle CPU

Computer Architecture Project

I designed and implemented a multicycle CPU following a custom insturction set. The architecture handles 3 different instruction formats and supports 11 opcodes, including ALU operations (nand, add, sub, mult), memory access (lw, sw, addm), and control flow operations (beq, jmp). This was developed in both Logisim and Verilog.

Flood & Fire Dashboard

Orange County Public Works, OC Survey

In this internship, I utilized LiDAR, drones, and total stations to collect GIS data, then used Python and ArcGIS to automate the processing and visualization of the data. I then created dashboards to inform the public about floor and fire risks in the OC area, which was cited in an IEEE research paper.
